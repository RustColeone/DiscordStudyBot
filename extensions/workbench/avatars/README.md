# Classmates' pictures

The Classroom shows each classmate with her own picture: the students module's (`extensions/students/<name>/avatar.png`,
given to every page through the core). A picture dropped here, named after her (`gpt.png`, `claude.png`,
`deepseek.png`, `gemini.png`, `grok.png`, or `you.png` for you), wins over hers, for this page only. PNG, JPEG, WebP
or GIF, square works best. Without any, a classmate gets a coloured circle with her initial.
